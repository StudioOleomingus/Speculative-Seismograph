# **Untitled\_Trinity Cardozo\_Suhaani Naik**

Untitled.Trinity Cardozo.Suhaani Naik

***“They are mutating.”***

***“The locusts and flies? Mutating. Whichever ones die and fall out of the Unsea have massive black eyes now. They’re adapting”***

Plaguegate. The Church was going mad about it, the general public simply uncertain about it. **Everytime a billionaire comes to existence and the cycle of the 10 plagues of Egypt ravage the land.**

When this first happened the people of Egypt were evacuated \- eventually, the neighboring countries taking in all the refugees. The straight borders of Egypt now highlighted in sharper contrast.

Eventually the Plagues compounded. Cycles over cycles of the same 10 Plagues over and over.

A wall of almost sentient blackness, occasionally lit up by the fall of burning hail. The meagre light offered swallowed by swarms upon swarms of locusts. 

Chirping and buzzing. The sound akin to crashing waves.

The once lifeblood of Egypt now. Quite literally. Lifeblood. The river was a fresh flood of blood, rich and red and very unsanitary. But the blood receiving patients didn’t seem to (know) mind too much. B+ blood is excessive already. O- would have been the ideal blood river type, but the gods are never that kind.

**“Lo\! Cust Protein is set to become one of the largest food producers and manufacturers in history\! CEO Unneth Ikal is one of the youngest billionaires in the current day\! Forbes 40 Under 4—”**

Researchers stopped counting the plagues, they kept overlapping so much. The frogs and flies were treated the same way. An unending, free source of food. 

**An infinite money glitch. The Church praised Unneth and other CEO’s who were willing to show God that even when we are punished, we will make something good out of it.** Parents echoed this sentiment to their children.

One phase of the plague doesn’t even end before another starts. A writhing concoction of blood, lice, flies, frogs and inky darkness made the Unsea. The Unseas previous government fought to maintain ownership of it and its near endless resources, they unfortunately lost the battle.

The Unsea is capitalized on, and another cycle of the Plagues breaks out. The hard border drawn once with pen on paper, now feels like it was drawn by God themself. A physical proof that even God endorses Egypt's ruler drawn border. It has become a tourist attraction, bought by Datney, 

***“Come\! You can never unsee this Unsea\! Those exposed to the Unsea often find themselves with symptoms of boils, lice and death upon entry, do NOT enter the Unsea, T\&C applied”***

Conspiracy theorists rave and rant about the Plaguegate meter

“The existence of Billionaires is single handedly the cause of the Unsea\!” They cry when the locusts swarms seem particularly thick. The investigative agencies hide the true reason behind this intervention, a perfect Pinocchio.

The flies, lice and locusts form their own ecosystem in the darkness, immune to the bacteria, developing immunity to the dark. The Unsea blocks critical trade routes, and the petroleum within it is worth millions simply due to its origin. Underground caverns are built for harvesting petrol, but God save anyone caught by those diabolical mutated locusts.

The burning hail stones from the Unsea are priceless jewellery, even God could not think up of the ways human greed can consume. Our planet may not be eternal, but control over the Unsea is control over the world, and the Unsea is as eternal as God. As eternal as the rich. Billionaires actively stop going in public so as to limit their causing of more plagues, others shouldn't be getting richer off my investment.

Others give away just enough money so as to not be billionaires anymore. 999 million so that another undeserving protein brick brand doesn't get rich quick.

The name of the land Pre-Unsea is wiped from records, Plaguegate and the Unsea remains.

**Your family is from the Unsea? Do you have the…yknow…boils?**
