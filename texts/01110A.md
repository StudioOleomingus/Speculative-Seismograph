**Constitutional Preamble**

By this communal declaration, wholly and agreeably asserted to, under no duress We the people of the Earth , solemnly swear to uphold a true and absolute democracy,  
A world of just ownership, A world that bridges the gap between those who produce and those who reap the benefits. A world where we no longer hide away the sweat of our economies in basements, sweatshops and factories. A true embrace, We shall open up our homes, our closets, our lawns and backyards, We shall eat from the same bowl of cereal , lay on the same couch and shit in the same toilet. Relationships shall be built anew. Let us move into this world not as strangers but as forever coinhabitants of a shared planet.

**Article I**

Any person who uses, consumes, or possesses a product, service, or system created through the labor of another shall thereby grant to such laborer or laborers an irrevocable claim equal to one-sixteenth (1/16) of the user’s inhabitable living space.

The laborer may, at their discretion, occupy or make use of the portion so claimed.

The disposal, transfer, or destruction of the product or service shall not extinguish this right.

This Article shall apply without exception.

**Article II**

Any owner of fractional space receives the right to place any objects upon fractional spaces owned by them

Article 1 applies to all fractional spaces and upon the fractional spaces owned by those in fractional spaces and so on and so forth.

The multiplication of fractional claims shall not be considered as a serious complaint

**Article III**

Any person entering or occupying a space in which they hold fractional claim shall be recognized not as guest, intruder, or tenant, but as a lawful co-inhabitant of that dwelling.

No host shall deny the presence of a rightful fractional claimant.

Food, furniture, sanitation, and rest shall be regarded as common utilities of the shared dwelling. Any rightful inhabitant may partake of nourishment, seating, repose, or relief without accusation of trespass.

**Article IV**

No boundary of nation, contract, lease, or custom shall supersede the rights established herein.
