**Untitled.Aman**

**Humans are entirely predictable.** 

*The ABSOLUTE has predicted that you would become a weightlifting champion. Your gym membership was purchased before you asked for one. Your diet was scheduled and your groceries purchased before you felt hungry. You paid for your coach before you knew you needed one.*

**You simply walked into the life already prepared for you. This is the convenience of the** **ABSOLUTE.** 

*We study everything: your pauses, your hesitations, the way your eyes linger, the way your habits repeat. From these fragments it calculates the most probable future for every person. And we are never wrong.*

**Your life was predicted before you were born** by using your mother’s history, your neighborhood, your social class, the opportunities and resources around you. 

Some of your futures are inconvenient but such is the frailty of life. *At age seven, you are informed that your right hand will be lost at thirty. So it is removed early and replaced with a prosthetic.*

*The ABSOLUTE predicts that your father will die in a car accident next Tuesday. You still drive him to work on Monday.*

*At age nine, you are shown the exact moment you will laugh during your father’s funeral. You spend decades wondering why.*

*Your partner receives a report stating she will eventually marry someone else. The relationship ends five years early, not because it failed, but because the future has already been calculated.*

**You can participate in your own funeral procession, who will cry if you die in the future,** what will the world around you do if you cease to exist. What it would be like to see what happens around you after you die.

The ABSOLUTE never commands.It only predicts. **But predictions with a 99.99999% success rate are instructions.**

Schools educate children according to their predicted talents. Employers recruit the futures they need.Entire lives are scheduled in advance so that the predicted version of reality arrives smoothly.

Decisions become unnecessary. Uncertainty disappears. **People stop asking what they want.They ask what the ABSOLUTE already knows \- as they once asked its predecessor the GPT.**

No government controls it. No company owns it. The world simply became too dependent on being right. And strangely, no one feels controlled.They feel relieved.

**Because thinking about the future is difficult. The ABSOLUTE already did it for you.**

If recommendation systems on platforms like YouTube, Instagram, or Zomato constantly predict what we will want next, shaping our choices through personalized feeds and suggestions \- The ABSOLUTE is only the logical extension of systems we already trust today.
