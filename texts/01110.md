# **Untitled.Ansh\_Parisha**

**Untitled.Ansh.Parisha**

The world is facing a catastrophic increase in methane emissions, a **62% rise**, stemming just from massive scale cattle farming. 

Milk, the nectar of the gods, has been a greenhouse noose \- production on such a large scale with just too many cattle farts for the world to be able to sustain\!

Since the demand for milk, cheese and paneer cannot possibly be quenched \-  milk production is a worldwide problem.

Even siloing and quarantining the animals has been of no help \- since it invites protests from the animal rights activists and the few vegans still persisting. 

So, **the World Nation** and **LEO (Lunar Exploration Organization)** have introduced a brand new organisation, **MILO (Milk Induced Lunar Orbital project)**, which will take care of milk production on the moon.

This began with a careful inoculation of of cows, buffaloes, and goats, donkeys and camels by examining them based on their health, and sending them to the Moon to produce milk there. 

Safe to fart on the moon, where these creatures can be found gamboling in low gravity environments \- we can now simply reap the benefits of a Methane free atmosphere and the goodness of natural milk.

This initiative demonstrates how global environmental challenges can be addressed through technological advancement and planetary collaboration\! With **MILO managing milk production** and integrating with geriatric organisations like ISRO \- to revitalise their old crafts as milk carriers \- humanity ensures access to essential nutrition while reducing the environmental impact of cattle farming on Earth.

**The whole project is an evident proof of the fact that we can indeed engineer our way out of any crisis \- perhaps even the crisis of being irrefutably human.**
