








# **Speculation for an Unsettled Planet.**

A collection of provocations by the students of Interaction Design at Anant National University, created as part of their Speculative Design course, Semester 06, 2026. 

The course introduced speculative design methods to challenge normative design and explore the systemic inequities that are often undergirded by an unquestioning adherence to conventional practice.

The works collected here are specifically oriented away from using speculation to generate escapist futurism, and instead the students used their speculations to understand power structures, explore hidden assumptions, and imagine alternative possibilities grounded in sustained critique. 

The course emphasized ethical practice even within the realm of speculative design, particularly addressing the risks of colonial speculation, exotification, and tech-solutionism.

**With the works compiled here students seek to demonstrate how design has always served ideology, and ask : who benefits from the precarious status quo of a fundamentally unsettled planet?**
