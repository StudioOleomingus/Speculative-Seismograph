







#Untitled.01101A
*By Sai & Vidya*

Why must we cling to antiquated forms of production, One that demands so much of so many

For long we've aspired to the Lockean ideals of labour and ownership. Let us now turn our attention inward, to the fabulous machine of desires but no longer beholden to the desires themselves but as a factory that can materialise them.

**A factory that operates on a flawless trimester cycle.** **A factory that shall let us dissolve the tyranny of wage labor and return to the original labor the kind that arrives with breath, sweat, and a midwife.** A closed loop system that would rival any supply chain Tim Cook can cook up

If one needs to listen to some music, just pop a seed and in a few weeks, You can plug in what you plugged out

Or if you want to build a house, just swallow any one of our premium house collection seeds, Take a few microdoses of concrete, wood and steel, Carry it in your stomach for a short period of 9 months or less and then gloriously deliver it\!, Is there any greater joy than living in a house that you've just given birth to.

And not just houses, but shoes or cars or a gummy worm and a transformer and toys that your children can play with all gestated into existence. The dinner you’ve been planning for next thursday. Why make it, pop a pill today and birth it just in time.

How might we make apparent the sheer effort and material cost incurred into the production of objects that are around us and taken for granted, and push back against planned obsolescence. 

**Through this scenario we're trying to think through this shift of attitudes and values, By bringing production into an intimate space \- staring into the sanctum of human-production.** Would you be alright with giving birth to the beautiful lime tiles that are used in your bathroom if it means having to burp out toxic fumes if not then are you alright with someone mining and processing the same? *Will you be more or less ok with throwing away an object you gave birth to?*
