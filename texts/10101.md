# **Untitled\_Vaishnavi\_Priyanshi**

**Untitled.Vaishnavi.Priyanshi**

In a world that needs a constant stream of stateless bodies to justify its borders a  steady supply of stateless men has been godsent\!

**Now that the government has combined  brothels with deportation centers, both places that live just outside the bounds of respectability can feed into  a virtuous loop.** Brothels lure customers who are then promptly deported or conscripted into stateless mercenary armies. 

Apsaras are independent recruiters operating inside brothels, hired or funded by the government or international organizations. Every time they come across a particularly adept candidate who goes through the service, an invisible transaction takes place. The citizenship of the customer is seized. From that moment onwards, the man becomes stateless, a citizen of nowhere. They are reclassified as gigolos. Stateless men no longer have rights, protection or any legal evidence of their existence. They are ghost mercenaries at the mercy of their employers.

Every day, the government collects thousands of such unsuspecting gigolos. Their passports , Birth certificates, domicile certificates, bank details, pan card, Aadhaar card are all systematically destroyed. 

As stateless denizens Gigolos become useful resources to wage illegal wars and massacres, to arms deal weapons and smuggle illegal drugs across borders.

WHO has now authorised the use of gigolos for tentative organ extraction and medical experiments, the UN utilizes gigolos for extreme labor. 

Since governments already control large digital identity systems like passports, biometric IDs, and national databases \-  citizenship becomes mostly digital, simply deleting someone’s data erases their legal existence with ease. Millions of people in the world are already stateless, adding a few million to that number might not even be a noticeable shift. 

The unsurveilled portals of brothels are an  ideal place to conduct such secretive deportations from \- **protected from the opprobrium of society and veiled from the gaze of civility perhaps we can club all our national sins into a single sanctum.**
