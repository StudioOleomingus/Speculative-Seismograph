*Why do we want to build a good **career**? Why **study** that much? Why **grind**? Why go through all this hassle of **work and pressure**? For a stable and **better future?** Well, what if you assure that promise of a better future in the **present**, but with certain... **trade-offs?** Perhaps rather **extreme** ones? And what if it has become **the norm**?*

A **dystopian** future where the **work culture** has broken past all limits, and productivity is the ULTIMATE sign of success and validation. T**he system is based on promises of a “better future” based on how much you sacrifice in the present.** The system pays in Future Promise Credits (FPCs) and there is a digital market and banking system. Workers not being able to be there for their loved ones can outsource an **AI version** of themselves from the “market” to be there for them.

The Banks provide loans that take away an existing part of what makes you, you. Your organs, your emotions, your sense of being you, is all mortgageable \- given as collateral, You will get this back only after the loan is paid in full.

*A person loans out good futures for their children in exchange \- forgetting all their collective good memories, and the love that they share for him, for the period of the loan.* 

This means that the children are taken care of, and the worker can operate without the burden of having to spend time with their family..

Or imagine a person who loans out a minimum threshold of work that the AI will do for him \- against the exchange of his arm. Which is replaced by a prosthetic one. A simple and fair tradeoff for that extra boost of productivity. 

The mounting work pressure that keeps accumulating with exceeding demands and ever-growing population has led to us taking extreme measures like these to meet them. **Our bodies don't have a choice but to "keep up" with it. At least this ensures our future is secure.**
