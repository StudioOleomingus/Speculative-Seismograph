







#Untitled.01011
*By Yadhu*

*Why build a good **career**? Why study so hard, grind so relentlessly, endure all this work and pressure? For a stable, better future? Well — what if that promise could be delivered now, in the present, for certain trade-offs? Rather extreme ones? And what if it had become the norm?*

This is a future where work culture has broken past every limit, and productivity is the ultimate sign of success and validation. The system runs on a single promise — a better future, paid out in proportion to how much you sacrifice in the present. It pays in **Future Promise Credits (FPCs)**, traded through a dedicated market and banking system. Workers too busy to be there for their loved ones can simply lease an **AI version** of themselves from the market to stand in.

The banks offer loans secured against the very things that make you *you*. Your organs, your emotions, your sense of self — all mortgageable, pledged as collateral and returned only once the loan is repaid in full.

*A parent loans out a good future for their children, and in exchange forgets, for the term of the loan, every shared memory and all the love between them.* The children are provided for, and the worker can labour on, unburdened by the pull of family.

Or picture someone who offloads a minimum quota of work onto the AI, paying with an arm — replaced by a prosthetic. A simple, fair trade for that extra boost of output.

Mounting work pressure, ever-rising demand, an ever-growing population: these are what drive us to measures like these. **Our bodies have no choice but to keep up. At least this way, our future is secure.**
