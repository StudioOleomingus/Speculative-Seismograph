**Untitled.Gayatri.Mihika**  
 

**Each year, to balance the planet’s carbon debt, the algorithm deletes identities.** 

**The planet has reached Maximum Capacity\!** Breathing is no longer a birthright, it is licensed. Through a resource management protocol called the Legacy Lottery, we now decide who is allowed to remain.

Aadhaar has evolved into a Hereditary Debt Ledger. It no longer confirms identity, it calculates eligibility. ***Every family carries a generational carbon score of water wasted, land extracted, energy consumed which is permanently archived.*** 

Justice is no longer moral \- that antiquated way no longer serves us well. It is instead perfect, illogical and mathematical. Because of this, you are born into your ancestors' karmas where a "Clean Lineage" enters the lottery once, while "High Debt" families (those whose   ancestors over consumed water, land, or energy) are entered a 100 times. **You do not inherit property, you inherit liability. So that the system does not punish individuals. It corrects bloodlines.**

When the algorithm selects an identity for deletion, the correction is absolute. The individual is vacated. Biometrics erased. Digital records scrubbed. Legal personhood dissolved. The grid forgets you. A newborn eventually fills the vacancy. Equilibrium restored.

Those who are deleted are no longer discussed. **The Lottery is not a court , it is a system check.** There are those among us who suffer the burden of overconsumption and lose their identities. We do not know what happens to them once they are stripped of their names, but we are no longer bothered by their existence since a newborn is already waiting to claim their vacated position.

The Legacy Lottery is a systemic judgment of your lineage. **Your existence no longer is a burden on the planet and for the first time in human history, you can begin to pay it back.** 

The state regulates life so that life may continue. Whether you remain or are replaced by a lower-debt successor, you serve the Equilibrium. Your existence is not guaranteed, it is a calculated risk to be managed till you persist and then replaced when you perish \- **allowing life to flourish on an oversaturated world \- the ultimate expression of progress.**
