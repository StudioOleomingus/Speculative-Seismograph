*How Voting Works?*

After breakfast, citizens visit the nearest Baithak Voting complex. 

Each **toilet stall represents a political party.** You enter the stall of the party you support and participate in the most natural democratic act possible. Sensors record usage.

Usage becomes votes.

If you trust none of them, you enter the neutral stall and flush empty.

A silent protest.

A clean rejection.

Public restrooms become spotless.
