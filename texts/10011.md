# **UPA\_Aarchi**

**Untitled.Aarchi**

**The Useless Profession Authority (UPA)** The Useless Profession Authority (UPA), is a valuable government body responsible for creating and managing jobs that do not actually need to exist.

The idea comes from observing how automation is increasingly taking over tasks that were once done by humans. Automatic doors open by themselves, elevators announce floors automatically, self-checkout machines scan items, and motion sensors turn lights on without anyone touching a switch.

**What happens to human jobs when machines begin doing most of the work?**

*The UPA provides one possible response to this situation \- the only one really\!* Instead of allowing unemployment to increase as automation expands, the authority creates new professions where people manually replicate or confirm tasks that machines already perform. The goal of the system is not efficiency or productivity. **Instead, it is simply to ensure that people remain “employed.”** 

Within this system, workers are assigned extremely specific roles. For example, someone might work as an Automatic Door Motion Observer, standing near an automatic door to confirm that it opened correctly. Another worker might be an Elevator Announcement Repeater, repeating the floor announcement that the elevator already makes automatically. Other roles include Escalator Step Movement Supervisors, who observe escalators to ensure they are moving, or Auto-Light Activation Witnesses, who confirm that motion sensor lights have turned on. 

These professions do not actually solve any problems because the machines already perform the tasks perfectly well. However, within the UPA system these roles are still treated as legitimate jobs.

**Workers are paid through a micro-task wage system. Instead of receiving a monthly salary,** workers earn small payments for each task they complete. For example, they might receive a small amount of money each time they confirm a door opening, observe an escalator cycle, or repeat an elevator announcement. 

Although the payments are very small, workers are still officially categorized as economically active citizens. This project visualizes the system through artifacts: a UPA Worker Identification Card and a UPA Wage Receipt. 

The ID card represents official employment within the authority, displaying the worker’s name, job title, and wage tier \- a final marker of the dignity of being employed given to you by your beloved government that still cares about the appearance of labour.
