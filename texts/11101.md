*What's your source of information?    Friends? Social Media? Gossip? That lizard sitting on your kitchen cabinet?  Or perhaps that strange old artifact from the yesteryears \- the News?*

**Traditional News Mediums** like News Channels, Newspapers, Magazines, and even Radio **are now out of commission**. Due to the competition **against other more accessible mediums such as social media.** People now don't make time to know about current news and the Traditional Mediums had no choice but to shut down.

Those few who were interested in news and preferred the traditional medium agreed to work in secrecy to deliver information, much like whistleblowers of news. **Undisclosed News works with the government to deliver information** as they realise that consumers aren't willing to read newspapers or watch news.

**How you may ask?**

Simply by outsourcing their information to seemingly unsuspecting influencers. **Now, the factual information is turned into a guessing game : spot the 1 truth among 4 lies**. Now that they are being lied to, consumers are now forced to ponder the news for the first time.

We want readers/consumers to realize the importance in discerning crucial information from noise. And how better to do so than to speak to them in the only language that seems to proliferate on the internet \- Lies\! 

We think our short attention span as well as our reluctance to pay for shared and fundamental truths about the world will inadvertently affect our ability to agree upon the state of the world we all share.  And if news traces the shape of reality, then the misinformation laden media we consume might have already started to alter the contours of our world.

**So here we are asking, why not learn to lie with precision \- challenge the very doctrine that seems to hold aloft every chatroom and reddit thread.
