
Kesavan Mama is truly plain in all senses of the word, He would be what comes to mind when you ask someone to think of an old man with abnormal ears, He would arrive in a chariot pulled by an elephant and a horse in tandem, One raises it's foot as the other one puts it down, Neighing and Trumpting alternatively to wake the villagers from their seista after lunch. And for him the jute carpet would already be laid out as he walks towards the Aal maram,  from his armpits he'll pull 100 ruppe notes along with toffees that get thrown out into thick crowd gathered to venerate him. Once under the banyan tree, he would seat himself on the familar spot where the laterite has made an indent for his buttocks after years of sitting, And it is from here that he'll tell you about the time a bomb fell on his house during the war , and how he had to pick it up and carry it to the nearest astrologer to defuse it. Or how when he was young and playing throwball with his friends . the ball went through the window of a neighbouring house and knocked out a cobra that was about to pounce on an unassuming young woman.  He recalls having to lecture the cobra to hammer into him that young woman are not worth his time and that he needs to be serious about his life and work, if he wishes his name to be uttered among other great and king cobras. "Look at my twisted tounge" he'd say, "Do u think I was born with this tounge, This tounge comes from years of twisting sentences, in wringing them extracting that ever so rich potent juice. that I pour into the mouths of those around me" "Giddy up! Good boy, you are young and you have much to look forward to."
